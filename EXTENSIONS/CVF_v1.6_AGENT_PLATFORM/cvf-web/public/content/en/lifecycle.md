# Work lifecycle

CVF presents five core stages in plain language. The Local lifecycle retains seven decisions; planning includes the specification and work-order decisions.

1. **Understand** (INTAKE): agree on the goal, scope, limits and stop conditions.
2. **Plan** (DESIGN, SPEC, WORK ORDER): shape the approach, make the expected result reviewable and assign the work with its boundaries.
3. **Build** (BUILD): carry out the admitted work within those boundaries.
4. **Check** (REVIEW): assess the evidence, result, unresolved issues and remaining work.
5. **Close** (FREEZE): record the accepted state and any owned remainder. The operator accepts the result and decides external effects.

A stage or role name alone does not grant permission. The receiving agent follows the governing Local rules and stops for decisions outside its authority. See [risk model](risk-model.md) and [start a specification](/home).
