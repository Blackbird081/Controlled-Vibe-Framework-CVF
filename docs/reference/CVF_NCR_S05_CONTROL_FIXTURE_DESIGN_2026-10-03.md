### Attempt policy

P1 (blocked-attempt logging) remains the selected design policy: every library request attempt blocked before sending is logged as an attempt and kept apart from successful connections, and execution requires zero socket egress by an enforcement mechanism that is not yet proven. P0 (strict zero attempts) needs the O3 forwarding change or an upstream alternative under its own admission. An empty success log proves neither zero attempts nor zero socket egress.
